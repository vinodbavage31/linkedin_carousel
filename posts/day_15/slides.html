The Lazy Algorithm That Actually Works

Day 15 of 100 Days of Machine Learning
What is K-Nearest Neighbors?
KNN is a supervised algorithm for classification and regression.

Instead of learning a complex model, it predicts by finding the K closest data points to a new sample.
How Distance Works
KNN measures similarity usually via Euclidean Distance.

The closest neighbors have the greatest influence on the final prediction.
Impact of K Value
Small K (e.g. K=1)
• Sensitive to noise
• High Variance
• Can overfit
Optimal K
• Balanced boundary
• Best tradeoff
• Good generalization
Large K
• Highly smoothed
• High Bias
• Can underfit
Curse of Dimensionality 🌌
As the number of features (dimensions) increases, the distance between data points becomes almost equivalent. KNN loses its effectiveness in high-dimensional spaces.

High-D (Sparse)

Python Implementation
from sklearn.neighbors import KNeighborsClassifier

# 1. Create
model = KNeighborsClassifier(n_neighbors=5)
# 2. Train
model.fit(X_train, y_train)
# 3. Predict
predictions = model.predict(X_test)
Advantages vs Disadvantages

Advantages ✅

  • ✔ Easy to understand
  • ✔ No explicit training phase
  • ✔ Works well on small datasets
  • ✔ Good for nonlinear boundaries

Disadvantages ❌

  • ✖ Slow predictions (large datasets)
  • ✖ Sensitive to feature scaling
  • ✖ Affected by noisy data
  • ✖ Poor high-dimensional performance
