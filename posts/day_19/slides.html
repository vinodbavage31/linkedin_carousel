Section 1 — Ball Rolling Analogy
Gradient Descent updates model parameters to minimize error. Think of a ball rolling down a hill toward the lowest point.
Section 2 — Learning Rate Visual
The size of each step is the learning rate.
Too Small
Very slow learning
Optimal
Fast convergence
Too Large
Overshoots minimum
Section 3 — Batch vs SGD vs Mini-Batch
Batch Gradient Descent
Uses all data • Stable but slow
Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD)
Uses one sample at a time • Fast but noisy
Mini-Batch Gradient Descent
Uses small batches • Best balance of speed and stability
