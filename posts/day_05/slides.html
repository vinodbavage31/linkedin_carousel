Linear Algebra in 1 Slide

Everything in Machine Learning is a matrix. Datasets are matrices. Images are matrices. Neural Networks are just a series of matrix multiplications. You don't need to multiply by hand, but you must know how dimensions work.

Statistics You Must Know

If you can't understand the data, you can't build a model. Statistics is the language of data. It tells you if your data is biased, how it's distributed, and whether your model's success was just luck.

What you actually need:
1
Mean & Variance
2
Distributions (Normal)
3

Calculus — Only This Bit

Models learn by minimizing error. Calculus helps us find the "bottom of the curve" (the lowest error). You don't need to solve complex integrals. You just need to understand slopes.

What you actually need:
1
Derivatives (Slopes)
2
Partial Derivatives
3
The Chain Rule
