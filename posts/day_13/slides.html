What is a Decision Tree?
A Decision Tree is a supervised machine learning algorithm that predicts outcomes by repeatedly asking the best possible questions.

Pros & Cons

Advantages ✅

  • ✔ Easy to understand
  • ✔ No scaling required
  • ✔ Nonlinear relationships
  • ✔ Classification & regression
  • ✔ Feature importance

Disadvantages ❌

  • ✖ Can easily overfit
  • ✖ Sensitive to noise
  • ✖ Unstable to small changes
  • ✖ Greedy algorithm
