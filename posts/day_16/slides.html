What is SVM?
SVM finds the optimal boundary (hyperplane) that separates different classes with the maximum possible margin.
Margin Concept
Small Margin ❌
Large Margin ✅
Larger margins usually lead to better generalization.
The Kernel Trick 🪄
If data cannot be separated in 2D, SVM projects it into a higher dimension where separation becomes possible!
2D (Non-separable)
→
3D (Separable)
When SVM Beats Deep Learning
  • ✅ Small datasets
  • ✅ High-dimensional data
  • ✅ Text classification
  • ✅ Limited compute
  • ✅ Clear decision boundaries
Deep Learning needs massive data. SVM often shines when data is limited.
