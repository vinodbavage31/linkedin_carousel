Why is it called "Naive"?
Naive Bayes assumes all features are completely independent from each other.
Example: Spam Detection
"free"
"offer"
"winner"
The algorithm treats these words as independent signals.
This assumption is often wrong but surprisingly effective.
Text Classification Example

When It Outperforms
  • Spam filtering
  • Sentiment analysis
  • News categorization
  • Document classification
  • Small datasets
  • High-dimensional text data
Naive Bayes often beats more complex models on text-based problems.
