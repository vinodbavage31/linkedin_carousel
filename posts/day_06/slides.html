Bucket 01
Supervised Learning

You have the answers (labels). You teach the model to map inputs to those answers so it can predict future unseen inputs. This is 90% of enterprise ML.

Classic Examples:
🏠
House Price Prediction (Regression)
Input: Bedrooms, Square footage ⟶ Output: Price ($)
📧
Spam Detection (Classification)
Input: Email text ⟶ Output: "Spam" or "Not Spam"

Bucket 02
Unsupervised Learning

You just have data. No answers. The model's job is to find hidden structure, groups, or anomalies inside the data entirely on its own.

Classic Examples:
👥
Customer Segmentation (Clustering)
Input: Purchase history ⟶ Output: Grouped into distinct buyer personas.
💳
Fraud Detection (Anomaly Detection)
Input: Millions of transactions ⟶ Output: Flag the 0.01% that look weird.

Bucket 03
Reinforcement Learning (RL)

Learning by trial and error. An "agent" takes actions in an environment to maximize a "reward". It learns the best strategy over time.

Classic Examples:
🎮
Game Playing Agents
AlphaGo beating the world champion at the game of Go.
🚗
Self-Driving Cars & Robotics
Reward: Staying on the road. Penalty: Crashing.
